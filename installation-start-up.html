Generator Installation Services

We begin with an on-site visit to review your property, evaluate electrical service, and understand your backup power goals. Then we prepare a detailed proposal.

Once approved we coordinate permitting, scheduling, and installation. When complete, the system is tested, verified, and ready for reliable operation.

  • • Complete system installation
  • • Permitting paperwork
  • • Load calculations
  • • Fuel system coordination
  • • Inspection scheduling
  • • Warranty setup
  • • Customer walk-through

Start-Up & Commissioning

Available for generators not installed by our team. We inspect, test, and verify the system so it is ready for service.

Do not assume a generator is ready just because it is installed. Proper start-up and verification matter.

  • • Initial system start-up and full inspection
  • • Voltage and frequency verification
  • • Transfer switch operation confirmation
  • • Control system setup
  • • Functional testing under load
  • • Warranty setup verification
